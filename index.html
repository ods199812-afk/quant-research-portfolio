01 / Approach

Research grounded in markets

My academic training combines economics and finance with quantitative methods. I'm especially interested in liquid global markets—including futures, rates, equity indices, and commodities—and in systematic approaches to studying changing market conditions.

I prefer research that begins with an economic or market hypothesis, translates it into measurable variables, and then tests its robustness out of sample. Mathematical models are tools for understanding and decision-making, not ends in themselves.

This site is an expanded companion to my résumé. It documents selected research projects, the methods I used, and the questions that remain open.

Current research interests

  • Time-series prediction and model stability
  • Regime detection and volatility dynamics
  • Multi-asset allocation and risk management
  • Systematic futures and macro strategies

PROJECT / 01

Market Return Prediction

Exploratory work on forecasting equity-market returns, feature design, and model stability using the Hull Tactical Kaggle dataset.

PythonLightGBMRidgeOut-of-sample testing
Research approach+

Research question

Can lagged returns, momentum, volatility, and other available signals support robust return forecasts?

What I explored

  • Time-ordered feature construction to avoid forward-looking information.
  • Comparison of linear and tree-based prediction approaches.
  • Out-of-sample evaluation, feature diagnostics, and sensitivity to changing regimes.

Status

Ongoing exploratory research; no live-performance claim.

PROJECT / 02

Financial Market Regime Modeling

Using Gaussian Hidden Markov Models to investigate latent volatility and market-state dynamics.

HMMState probabilitiesFilteringVolatility
Research approach+

Research question

How do hidden-state models characterize shifting market conditions, and what can be inferred without future observations?

What I explored

  • State-transition probabilities and regime-dependent observations.
  • Forward-backward inference, filtering versus smoothing.
  • Interpretation limits and possible integration with risk-aware trading workflows.

Status

Modeling prototype and methodological research; not a deployed live strategy.
